# Grid cliff siding: investigation (open)

P04 is reproduced with seed 2697992464 and the shared ReviewCam reconstruction. The existing wall consists of repeated 3 m by 4 m native modules with native inner/outer junction ownership. Sparse ground-rooted terrace stacks are a separate producer.

Alternatives:
- Placing whole study mountains against these faces would change the grid silhouette and leave unsupported intersections. Rejected.
- Rebuilding the canonical face/corner classifier would risk the accepted grass and slope joins. Unnecessary.
- Author compatible fractured rock siding against the existing measured module boundaries, then evaluate it in the photographed production scene. Retain exact grass caps and the canonical face ownership. Larger rooted terraces and wet-bank placement remain their separate subsequent reviews.

First experiment: carve fine irregular joints into the interior of each measured wall stock while pinning its complete joining boundary. All authoring runs offline. The experiment is not production acceptance: reject it if the repeated stock or artificial seams dominate the matched image.

## Owner direction: original cliff texture retained

The owner rejected the rock-textured siding on September 15: “lets stick with the original cliff side texture for now, and add rocks and green terraces/ledges and vines directly on the outside of the cliff face. try to match the style”. None of the siding prototypes was enabled in production. The native wall, corner, grass lip and palette remain authoritative.

Rejected experiments: small carved modules showed repeated seams; broad flat panels resembled paving; stepped panels made horizontal stripes; unbacked volumes left visible holes; backed/planted panels still conflicted with the native style. Float32 seam and closed-solid checks pass for the last offline geometry, but do not constitute visual acceptance. These assets and images are retained only as rejected authoring evidence.

Revised work: retain native cliff geometry/materials, replace symmetric terrace stacks with offset native outcrops of varied heights, and attach foliage to measured cap and wall surfaces. Native collisions and full-footprint support remain required. Dry and wet banks retain one face vocabulary; no hydraulic terrain alteration is proposed.

## Owner's 22:33 direction: mossy rock formations
The owner rejects terracing with smaller copies of the ordinary cliff tile. Use
UltimateNature Rock_Moss_1/4/7 as the form/style reference: vertical ribs, broken
outcrops and layered mossy rocks, covering nearly the whole cliff. Keep the
existing terrain and flat grass crowns and add fitted external rock geometry.
The native-tile candidate-01 is rejected qualitatively (pyramid rhythm and sparse
sprigs). The moss-rocks study compares real source rock geometry in the same
production snapshot before replacing any placement/collision contract.

## Final implementation
The moss-rock sequence and fresh production checks are recorded in [result.md](result.md). The native-tile hierarchy and prior fractured texture studies remain rejected directions; the final producer uses closed external mossy rock formations.
