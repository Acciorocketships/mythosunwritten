# Larger shallow rock inclusions — September 25 follow-up

Owner rejected the previous outcrops in `download.png`: rectangular repeated
blocks, visible flat tops and bottoms, too much projection, and too small.

The previous rocks were native assets, not generated rock shapes: Farmlands
Cliff_Large_01–03 on the slopes, Meadow rocks and Farmlands Cliff_Flat at the foot.
Their placement was procedural. The prior end taper changed their mesh ends,
but the endpoint/end-band test missed internal shelves and the blocky middle.
The earlier claim of completely buried caps was therefore too broad.

## Final changes

- Replace the three cliff masses on faces with native Farmlands
  RockMedium_01, 02, 03, 04 and 06. The asset lineup is saved alongside this file.
  The source boulders have different, irregular outlines without the repeated
  rectangular columns. Basal Meadow rocks keep their native meshes.
- Face assets become shallow inclusions: independent tangent-plane width/height
  and one metre total normal thickness. Their two ends recede smoothly into the
  backing; the middle retains the source asset's relief, silhouette and UVs.
- Primary face widths are 6–8.5 m; tight corners use 4.5 m faces spread around the turn, retrying the anchor height.
  Companion scale varies. Burial caps the front at roughly 0.4 m normal distance.
- Fit the real top/bottom triangles after scaling and rotation, including internal
  shelves, rather than just the outermost endpoints. Detached triangle samples
  live in `CliffSlopeRockCaps.gd`; regenerate with
  `tests/harness/bake_slope_rock_caps.gd`. The independent tests inspect the actual
  rendered mesh, not this fit metadata.
- Each cluster uses distinct asset IDs, chosen deterministically without
  replacement. Source-front samples reject buried or tiny visible fragments;
  placement retries another unused asset, then withdraws any singleton.
  `CliffSlopeRockFronts.gd` is generated by the same bake harness. Wider spacing
  inside the group lets different silhouettes read.
- Face rocks no longer raise an ellipsoid moss mound over themselves. Their
  material grows moss around the join, rather than covering the entire shallow
  face. Nonuniform transforms now use the correct normal matrix in the shader.

## Verification

The red-first inclusion test fails on the owner's previous state. The final two
inclusion tests and 34 slope tests pass: 36 tests. They cover
actual top and bottom triangle burial, depth, larger faces, different assets in
each cluster, corner retention, slope continuity, terrain seams, and basal roots.
Native control uses the same source meshes and production solid meshing. The
shelf check uses transformed geometric normals, accounting for nonuniform scale.

World review uses seed 2697992464, anchor (262,30,1045), and the exact camera of
the owner's annotated capture: (290,36,1028) toward (310,31,1002), FOV 62.
Additional front and side views check burial from other angles. The world was
loaded fresh, then its rock geometry was rebuilt through the review harness
after the final fitting changes; existing grass was retained. Saved detail,
front and side images show that final rebuild, not a second cold startup.

The complete project test suite was not run. Existing terrace grass edges and
visual-only rock collision status are outside this follow-up.
