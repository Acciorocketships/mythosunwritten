## Upper inhabited crossings

The old bridge proof rejected every span within two columns of any platform, at every height. On 101/large, 103/grand and 31/large this rejected most proposals and left zero source spans. The rule now protects the platform datum and lower approaches while allowing an upper street at or above the neighboring platform's bearing datum to undergo the normal complete endpoint-room and reserved-envelope proof. There is no seed exception.

Same four-town source survey: spans 0,0,0,1 -> 2,3,3,1. All four final fabrics validate. A first trial failed 103/grand because a covered market met a GARDEN_FLOOR above its canopy: the existing shared-interface exception recognized PUBLIC_FLOOR only. Both floor kinds now retain their already-sealed interface, without overwriting or bypassing occupancy checks.

The new regression checks three real upper towns for surviving composed spans, unchanged plinth outside explicitly reserved wall tunnels, fabric validation, zero floating masses and zero finished roof/public-air triangle intersections. Its first draft incorrectly forbade the pre-existing explicit wall tunnels; the revised oracle excludes only those exact bounded carved cells. Original red logs are retained. The old September29 platform suite still fails retired all-stone-asset/signature/no-inhabited-plinth assumptions; it is not reported as passing.
