Town material studies

Five coordinated primary and trim palettes on the same original Pure Village House_16c corner-tower prefab. Native geometry and source textures; these are controlled material experiments, not screenshots of the procedural generator. Click a building for its corner detail.

Oak, walnut and birch name visual tones rather than verified wood species. The two brown roofs combine the authored tile relief with existing wood grain. The warm stone option changes material and normal strength; a collegiate/Gothic building grammar is still separate work.
