## Crown structure

The new `crown_obligation_probe.gd` separates initial public thresholds from later feature contacts. The three five-storey source houses in 63/grand have **zero direct public doors on their highest floor**. Their adjacent balconies are `private_room_walkout`, with no stair connection to public circulation; later overhang-support reservations also touch their upper rooms. Some highest rooms cover public walks at distances 8–10 bands. Those are distant upper masses, not low tunnel ceilings.

This does not authorize deleting their balconies or support cells after sealing. It means the silhouette should be co-decided before optional feature placement, while preserving actual lower room and skywalk obligations. No late visual-only height trim has been introduced.
