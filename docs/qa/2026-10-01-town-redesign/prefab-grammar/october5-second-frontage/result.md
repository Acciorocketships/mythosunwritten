# Second projecting frontage on tall houses

Accepted as a limited improvement to facade depth. Houses with at least four storeys may fit a second complete projecting room on a different face and floor. All houses receive their first opportunity before the second pass. Existing measured roof, neighbour, public-air, lower bearing and upper closure checks apply unchanged. Shorter houses retain their single frontage; embedded wall rooms retain their earlier policy. No repeated vertical stack of attachments is added.

## Evidence

- Red-first five-storey fixture produced only one projection before the change. The final room-projection suite passes **9 tests / 77 assertions**, including disjoint envelopes, different faces, ground/top preservation, native returns/floors/brackets and obstruction refusals.
- Ten towns: projections **31 → 33**, with every previous host/band/direction/bounds record preserved. Added second fronts are on 63/grand house.010 and 103/grand bridge.00.end.1.lower. All tower records remain identical.
- Every published walk's ceiling array is identical, preserving all **482 covered quarters**. Floating-mass and public-air intrusion audits are zero in all ten towns.
- Final production-player routes pass **14/14**: six seed-63 skywalks in both directions, plus the seed-103 raised turn at (0,4,-1) both directions.
- Two attempted seed-63 probes at (2,3,-3) were invalid harness targets: first lacked two same-level published walk edges; the alternate access query lacked a published approach. Both stopped at route assertions before traversal and are excluded from pass counts. No conclusion about that cell's general accessibility is drawn.
- Matched native before/after close views for both added fronts inspected, with reverse-angle returns and brackets. They produce real inhabited wall offsets and complete native closures while preserving adjacent roofs. Seed-63 context views remain visibly too tall: this does not solve the overall apartment-like silhouette.

## Measurement and limits

`tall_frontage_probe.gd` now excludes covered party-wall slots using finished mass occupancy and native exposure checks; the previous version counted potential slots regardless of actual exterior exposure. This remains a wall-run diagnostic, not a screen-space aesthetic score.

The initial candidate reduced four-storey-or-longer exposed column runs 13→11 in seed 63 and 11→9 in seed 103; seed 31 stayed 12. The longest remaining run is still five storeys. The two-pass candidate has identical projection geometry as sets on this corpus (emission order differs). Its purpose is to preserve established first-pass opportunities before extra competition. An interim concern that the first candidate displaced a neighbour was disproved by the exact current-baseline comparison; it came from a stale earlier snapshot, and is not a claimed bug fix.

Next structural work must address the distribution of occupied floors and route landings that creates narrow shafts below wide crowns. More facade attachments alone will not meet the owner's massif and reference-building direction. Full redesign acceptance remains open.
