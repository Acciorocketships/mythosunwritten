# Native compounds selected by the town planner

The production program now registers 18 complete seeded cross-house derivations.
An offline vocabulary exporter derives five additional doorway-relative planner
profiles from their actual sealed recipe body, roof envelope and footing cells.
Six sampled derivations extend behind the supported frontage contract and are
excluded. The exporter compares against legacy profiles only, so rerunning it
does not consume its own generated profiles. Runtime planning uses constants,
not source asset imports or catalog inspection.

Native prefab landmarks bypass generic kit-house reconstruction. Their 106
native placements (in the observed small compound) retain their transforms,
collision flags and identities through final payload filtering. Native pitched
roofs advertise no upper sockets and reject a public floor at their box ceiling.

## Raised-site failure and correction

The first holdout survey selected a second compound in 61/grand at band one.
Actual-player ascent and descent both failed: the public landing was supported,
but the private stair arrival had no floor. The generic kit replacement path
had supplied support that the preserved native assembly does not own. A valid
source solid/bearing claim was therefore insufficient proof of rendered support.

Native selection and realization now both require their entire conservative
footing footprint at natural ground (`WarrenMassif.base_at`). Raised native sites
are disabled until a complete podium/private approach is part of their contract.
This restriction is specific to the new native grammar; existing elevated
buildings and public routes retain their previous rules. The failed walk remains
in evidence rather than being counted as a pass.

## Verification

- Native town integration: 3 tests / 222 assertions pass. Seed 211/grand retains
  the complete native assembly, introduces no generic duplicate, and every native
  part's measured box clears finished public walking air. The other tests protect
  the roof/public-floor boundary and the rejected raised-site regression.
- Independent asset-template oracle: 1 test / 267 assertions pass, 22 profiles.
- Existing complete-building variant coverage: 1 test / 200 assertions pass.
- Existing production real-terrain integration: 1 test / 125 assertions pass.
- Final survey: all 16 towns generate; 211/grand selects one native compound,
  retaining 106 parts with zero generic native replacements. The eight holdout
  towns select none after rejecting the unsupported raised site. This proves
  admission/preservation, not adequate distribution or broad visual variety.
- Actual player in full 211/grand payload: ascent and descent from its public
  landing through the private stair pass. This is exterior access to a closed
  door, not a traversable interior claim.
- Overview, overhead and close native renders inspected. The compound's joined
  roof, overhanging eaves, corner timbers and stone footing survive. Close framing
  crops the roof peak, so use the overhead/overview for the complete silhouette.

## Limits

This is a first production family, not full prefab-generalizing architecture.
The new compounds still have sparse upper facades; broader roof/facade art review,
more family types, corner towers, palette coordination and denser sampled variety
remain. Their body mask reserves a bounding rectangle, losing usable space inside
compound notches. Raised compounds require their own rendered support contract.
Native acceptance is only 1/16 in this survey. The existing town corpus still has
long roofs and flat faces outside this family; no overall art acceptance or full
suite/performance claim is made.
