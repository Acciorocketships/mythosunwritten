## Town trial and rejection

Registering eight seeded configurations added two measured profiles (40 to 48 derivations; 11 to 13 profiles). In 32 source plans, all build and 15 naturally select 22 instances. Finished-town checks in 5/grand and 7/grand pass 517 assertions for family presence, complete native-part preservation and public-air clearance. Both native entrances in 7/grand pass both directions (4/4). The custom town-front image is valid; some automatic cameras stood inside other buildings and are excluded.

However, the larger early reservations alter neighboring parcels and routes. In 31/large covered walk quarters decrease 34 to 24; in 8/grand, 70 to 60. Both retain zero floating and public-air intrusions, but this is an enclosure regression against the owner's stated priority. More tower sites in 8 do not compensate for lost covered streets. Default town registration is therefore withdrawn. The family and measured site/recipe API remain available for explicit studies; `export_native_vocabulary.gd -- --include-rear-jetty-study` enables the experimental vocabulary. A normal export restores the accepted 40 derivations/11 profiles. This option writes the vocabulary; it is not a runtime setting.

The candidate-only production-seed test is archived alongside its passing output rather than retained as an assertion against the restored vocabulary. Independent geometry and reservation regression tests remain active. No seed-specific placement, weaker clearance, or probability increase was used to conceal the regression.
