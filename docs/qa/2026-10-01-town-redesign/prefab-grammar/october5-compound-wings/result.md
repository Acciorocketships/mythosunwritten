# Compound crown wings, October 5

The stepped-wing rule now handles nonrectangular upper footprints. It can lower a complete roofable range while leaving a connected upper house. This is a measured improvement candidate, not completion of the town art review.

## Rule

Candidates must be fully contained in the original crown. The remaining crown must stay connected and decompose into ranges at least two modules wide. Lower rooms must bear the complete wing; doors, balcony bearings, abutted rooms, walked surfaces and external construction remain protected. Existing native roof assembly supplies the lower roof. No seed-specific placement or generic height cap was introduced.
