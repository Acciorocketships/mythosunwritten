# Supported cover-chain experiment — rejected

A candidate allowed a supported PLOT_OVER room to extend its original house
across another independently borne tunnel column. A finite outward pass retried
columns encountered before their neighbouring host room. Existing roof height,
edge profile, crown, jamb and final composition bearing checks were retained.

The host-selection/whole-cover suite passed 3 tests / 41 assertions. Eight holdout
towns built with zero floating-mass and public-air roof intrusions. However, no
holdout contained an admitted cover beyond the original host's adjacency. In
101/large the planner gained one such extension at (-2,4,2), but both it and its
parent cover remained absent in the finished town. 43/grand's candidate correctly
failed the edge profile. Representative finished coverage stayed 24 and 112 fine
quarters respectively. These results do not demonstrate architectural improvement.

A construction-time trace identified the 101 failure: the planned jamb at macro
(-1,1), band 6 remained ALLOCATABLE at fine (-2,6,2), rather than a room or claimed
stone. The neighbouring bridge-end jamb also lacks claimed bearing. The final
proof correctly refuses the overhead room and releases the unsupported crown.
Restricting the jamb check to the slab-facing half was also tested; it produced
no new coverage and was reverted. Do not weaken the bearing invariant.

All candidate production and test changes were removed. Candidate functions and
test are saved as .txt evidence, outside executable paths. No new art acceptance.
The next change must preserve inhabited jambs and connected rooms as one
construction decision; adding post-hoc source covers or stone piers repeats
already rejected approaches (see October 3 tunnel-bearing review).
