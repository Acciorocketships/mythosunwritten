Admission required continuous backing through every roof-height band, no addressed doors and existing roof-clearance checks. Eight structural tests/34 assertions pass, including short backing/doorway/forbidden rejection. Candidate reproduced in actual103/grand, replacing the small gable with a taller single slope.

Structural green tests do not prove art fidelity. Candidate rejected; BuildingDesigner restored byte-for-byte to the immediately preceding source.
