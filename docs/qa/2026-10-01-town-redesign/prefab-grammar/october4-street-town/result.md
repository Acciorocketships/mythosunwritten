# Native street-house family in generated towns

The large cross-house family alone appeared in only one of the 16 survey towns.
The existing prefab-derived StreetHouse_1 family now has a production site and
recipe adapter. Its narrower body preserves the authored projecting upper floor,
carved brackets, facade returns, coordinated gable/roof, and masonry footing.
The site adds exactly one scale-correct private stair, anchors by its real arrival,
and publishes its complete envelope and ground contacts. Shared placement and
recipe conversion retain the reflection fix and anisotropic lattice compensation.
No source GLBs are loaded by runtime planning, and no new bake was needed.

The offline vocabulary adds 16 seeded street derivations in two measured profiles
(two and three 3 m native bays). Together with the compounds there are 34 native
derivations, seven generated profiles, and 24 total legacy/native profiles.
The initial four-bay production trial produced the unwanted long, thin roof in
the actual town view. Four-bay and longer houses remain derivable study cases,
but are not production candidates until additional roof features break that run.

All native sites remain ground-supported; this does not solve raised native
podiums or invent upper route connections. Existing native reservations and
whole-assembly kit preservation apply to both families.

## Results

The final same-seed 16-town survey builds 16/16. Native houses appear in 12 towns
(previously one), with 16 native houses / 945 retained pieces and no generic
replacement duplicates. Seven of the eight holdout towns contain native houses.
211/grand retains its cross-house and gains a compact street house; the new family
does not merely replace the one successful compound example.

- Street site/recipe tests: 2 tests / 837 assertions pass, multiple lengths,
  seeds and world scales, complete brackets, one entry, positive runtime bases,
  valid baked compilation and rejected invalid inputs.
- Combined site, recipe and town regressions: 9 tests / 9793 assertions pass.
- Expanded whole-town preservation/public-air test includes both 211/grand and
  7/standard: 3 tests / 583 assertions pass after that expansion.
- Independent measured-template oracle: 1 / 291 passes. Complete variant
  grouping/coverage: 1 / 264 passes. Real-terrain integration: 1 / 125 passes.
  The latter is a correctness result, not performance acceptance.
- Actual player on native street-house fabric payloads: 8/8 ascent/descent walks
  at four orientations. Final 7/standard: 4/4 walks over both native entries.
  Holdout 61/grand: 2/2 walks. These reach the exterior closed door, not interiors.
- Final street-level and town overview renders inspected. The compact projected
  facade has visible structural depth and continuous roof/material interfaces.
  The broader town still has tall flat faces and exposed public decks; these
  views do not close the owner's full massif/architecture art requirements.

Still open: more prefab families and full reference reconstruction coverage,
raised native support, richer roof silhouettes, native palette sampling, town
canopy/enclosure review and final broad acceptance. No full-suite green claim.
