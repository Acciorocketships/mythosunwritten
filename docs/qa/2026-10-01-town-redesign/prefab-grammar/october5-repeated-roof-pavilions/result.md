# Repeated roof pavilions — October 5

Accepted a bounded roof articulation improvement. Long existing room ranges can carry several connected transverse pavilions, separated by real hall sections. Pavilion depth stays within the normal four-module native roof limit. Ordinary roof junctions, complete envelope checks and gable closure remain authoritative.

## Defect and rejected iteration

The old range rule added one pavilion, sometimes five modules deep, then left an arbitrarily long tail. The new regression over 12×4, 4×12 and 14×2 ranges failed before the change. Recursion now works on strictly smaller remaining hall pieces and leaves at least two hall bays between successive pavilions.

The first candidate shortened individual sections but aligned pavilions across neighboring crown strips. The existing collinear union then made two eight-bay roofs: the largest section in 31/large increased from 28 to 32 modules. That candidate was rejected. The accepted candidate checks already planned neighboring wings when choosing its pavilion position, preventing a new long collinear union. A separate eight-seed 10×8 compound-crown regression checks final joined roof depth and length. This is a planning heuristic; final geometry still undergoes the existing clearance and joining checks.

## Result

In 31/large the largest individual roof section falls from 28 to 16 modules. House 014 changes from a five-deep pavilion plus seven-long tail into three four-by-four sections with alternating ridge axes. House 015 uses staggered pavilions and shorter halls. The roof count rises 49→55 without tiny roofs, exposed open ends, gable holes, unsupported roof air, cut eaves or uncapped towers. In 103/grand maximum section area falls 20→16; 8/grand remains 16. Existing tiny-roof/gable-hole findings in these holdouts are unchanged, not fixed by this pass. Native public-air intrusions remain zero in all three detailed roof probes.

Across ten towns every one of the 480 previously covered walkway quarters retains its exact ceiling height. Floating masses and public-air intrusions remain zero. Turret counts are unchanged except 43/grand, which gains one supported corner turret (one→two) because the shorter roof envelope leaves space. The native two-sided turret views show its host roof palette and corner attachment. No additional tunnel is claimed by this roof pass.
