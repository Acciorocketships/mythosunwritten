# Stacked balcony bearings, October 5

The previous bracket-contact audit exposed two real missing bearings. In 63/grand, house 013 shifts its upper floor one module outward; in 103/grand, the merged bridge-end house does the same. An upper balcony's short diagonal brackets began below that projecting room, in the air above a lower balcony. The reservation grid labels the lower balcony headroom PRIVATE_VOLUME, so treating every such cell as a physical room hid the error.

`KitBalconySupports.seat` compares generated brackets with actual house floorplates. Where no room bears the diagonal's foot, a lower balcony deck within one storey can carry it instead. The diagonal reaches that deck rim and an upright closes the triangle beneath the upper balcony. Both are the existing Suntail timber post asset. It never invents a ground-reaching support, never uses a higher deck, rejects a public-air column, leaves ordinary wall-borne brackets unchanged, and is idempotent. Fitting precedes the measured bracket-contact protection used by stepped wings.

Five triangles are repaired in each affected town. Matched native before/after views show the former dangling diagonals replaced by connected gallery framing. Authored curved cantilever brackets, upper-room setbacks and both balcony decks remain. Measured native upright bounds start exactly at the lower deck and stop below the upper deck: no extension into the roof or a stone footing on the balcony.

Validation: four tests / 60 assertions pass, including both finished towns, lower-deck ownership, vertical reach limits, wall-bearing preservation, absent-bearing refusal and public-air exclusion. A subsequent focused native endpoint test passes ten assertions (three new measured-geometry assertions). Six-town survey fields are exactly equal to the previous survey; 220 overhead quarter samples are preserved, with zero floating/roof-air failures. Actual-player traversal passes all six directions/routes in 103's skywalk probe. One intermediate ray sample reports missing floor on a source-bridge endpoint; the actual continuous traversal completes in both directions, so this is not claimed as a complete ray-support audit.

This resolves the specific older balcony-bearing finding from the previous pass. It does not complete the broader silhouette/enclosure redesign: tall repeated wall shafts and exposed boardwalk runs remain visible.
