# Interior square trial — rejected, October 5

A candidate preferred eligible 3x3+ plaza sites with raw massif frontage on three sides, retaining existing support, landing and excavation limits. All 24 source towns sealed. Only 31/large changed: 2x2 at band 2 became 3x3 at band 2. Finished 31 and control 83 had zero floating/roof-air failures; surveyed street enclosure was unchanged. The candidate test passed 6 assertions and actual court access passed both ways.

Native views contradict the intended result: the plot still sits on the outer edge of the town, with open countryside on two sides. Raw massif columns do not establish final inhabited enclosure. It also reads as a railed grass platform rather than the requested broad deck inside a cluster. The candidate and its test are archived here and removed from production. No claim of delivered interior town squares is made.

Next: plan the court and surrounding occupied frontages together, preserving upper-level access and supporting construction, and verify against actual final rooms rather than raw massif height. The gallery eye view is obstructed by a rail; the overhead view is sufficient to reject the site. Separate source tunnel diagnosis shows missing adjacent host storeys and insufficient jambs remain common; no tunnel/skywalk or spire increase was implemented in this trial.
