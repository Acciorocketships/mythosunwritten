# Street crossings before optional bays; usable landing guards

Accepted two targeted rules. The ordinary-house height experiment is rejected again and both its production method and tests are restored byte-for-byte to the accepted raised-district policy.

## Bay arbitration

`WarrenSpatialFeatureSolver` reconstructs supported enclosed street-crossing prospects from complete room records, canonical public floor faces, existing structural cells and feature/daylight reservations. It uses the same candidate/admission and whole-shell envelope functions as the final skywalk compiler. Optional facade bays yield when their complete bounds occupy a prospect. Native roof, crown and passage checks remain authoritative later; this does not force an unsafe bridge into the finished town.

The ten-town corpus is 31/large and 8,9,13,43,53,63,83,103,301/grand. Covered walk quarters increase 468→476. In 43, a high peripheral candidate moves to a street crossing: four quarters gain an eight-band ceiling at ground and four gain a three-band ceiling on the raised street. In 8, a two-band crossing moves one fine bay along its two endpoint houses: two formerly covered quarters open and two neighboring quarters gain the same low cover. Its number of crossings and covered quarters are unchanged. All other eight towns retain exactly the same per-quarter ceilings. Every town has zero floating masses and zero public-air intrusions. Source plots and corner turret counts/placements are unchanged.

The changed bridge in 43 has a complete timber shell joining its two endpoint houses and a closed underside over the deck street. The moved projection on house.036 retains its host plaster/timber and supported base; both native side views were inspected. The low crossing in 8 has complete wall bearings and a closed underside; its roof/underpass views were inspected. Some automatic side cameras stand inside neighboring roofs and are not acceptance evidence.

## Physical landing defect

Actual-player checking found two blocked skywalks in 53. A source-restored baseline reproduces all four directional failures, so this is not caused by the height or bay changes. Collider attribution identifies `kit.spatial.feature.balcony.01/k0005` and `kit.spatial.feature.balcony.05/k0017`: native balcony end rails fence off accepted spans.

`KitSkywalkLandings.open_landings` opens only the deck boundary edges that meet accepted span endpoints at the same height, including both lanes of a two-lane bridge. Existing doorway openings and guards on every other side/height remain. Both rendered rail and its collision disappear through the ordinary deck assembler; collision is not disabled independently of the model. This runs on house terraces as well as feature balconies, after all masses exist and before assembly.

53 changes from 10/14 to 14/14 actual-player traversals. The two repaired spans are shown from their real walking heights in `53_grand_crossing0.png` and `53_grand_crossing2.png`: continuous floor and open framed ends, with side guards intact. 43 passes 14/14 and 8 passes 12/12. Total 40/40 includes skywalks, source bridges and bored underpasses, each in both directions.

## Rejected height experiment and remaining work

With the new bay priority, the ordinary optional three-storey cap restores the previously lost crossings in 31 and 53. But the six holdouts still lose genuine low cover in 8 (including bands 2–4) and 9 (band 6). That height policy is not accepted; `rejected-height-ten.json` retains the evidence. Ground/mixed-datum tall shafts need a joint room/circulation design rather than a global cap.

The full authored-prefab generalizing grammar, broad town-square distribution and overall art acceptance remain open. Existing source-prefab reconstruction benchmarks are not proof that arbitrary generated houses match authored quality. The remaining tall faces and dense roof junctions are visible in the native views; this report accepts the crossing and guard repairs only.
