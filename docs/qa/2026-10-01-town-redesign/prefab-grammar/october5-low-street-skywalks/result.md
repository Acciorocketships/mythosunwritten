# Closer street ceilings from competing skywalks

Accepted a selection rule for supported bridge-houses: among valid street-crossing candidates of the same width, choose the closer ceiling before seeded variety. Distance is measured from each crossed column’s nearest public floor beneath the span; the worst separation determines rank. Street-crossing and paired-width priority, real endpoint rooms, complete shell clearance, roof reservations, retained-crown occupancy and physical support are unchanged.
