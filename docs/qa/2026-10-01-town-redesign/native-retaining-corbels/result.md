The native stone module contains a projecting cap and curved stone brackets with a complete backing. Timber supports project approximately a full module and need an actual overhang; they were not scattered onto flat walls.

Baked the original stone support with its materials and trimesh collision using --keep-existing. KitRetainingRelief places whole, unscaled native modules under exposed retained crowns on sufficiently wide runs. This is an improvement to the retaining vocabulary, not final architectural acceptance.
