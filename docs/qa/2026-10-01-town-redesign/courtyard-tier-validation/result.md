# Courtyard tier validation

The previous integration check found four failed absolute-height assertions. Candidate tracing shows no eligible band-8 sites in 58/large or 58/grand. Temporarily omitting early secondary gates produces exactly the same selected sites, ruling out that proposed cause. A second trace finds no upper street landings from which such a court could be entered. Final source inspection establishes that both towns now have only a band-4 platform and streets no higher than band 4. Their courtyard is at the highest tier they actually generated. 13/grand still has a band-8 platform, upper streets and a band-8 courtyard.

This is an obsolete fixed-height fixture expectation, not proof of a missing courtyard or broken access. Nested tiers are seed- and available-area-dependent (`WarrenTownPlatform.nested_tiers`); forcing a second tier into these named layouts would contradict procedural variability. Tests now require the selected court to equal the highest generated platform bearing in these flat-ground examples, with that tier at least two storeys high. The explicit 13 nested fixture additionally retains the four-storey/band-8 minimum. Thus the tests still reject selecting a lower court when the generated upper tier exists. No production selection, gate or support algorithm was changed. Temporary tracing and gate experiment were restored byte-for-byte against their pre-probe copies.
