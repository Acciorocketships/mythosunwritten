# Native terrace hierarchy — retained partial improvement

Broad exposed faces now use complete 12 m native KayKit bases, with supported
8 m and 4 m shelves above them. Each stack shares one spatial reservation and
chunk owner. Upper shelves consume the actual native cap below; public space,
grading and wet feet still veto the complete placement. Exposed lower caps
reserve the upper footprint before ordinary grass is planted. Native proportions,
original terrain and water calculations remain unchanged.

The original regression has no broad bases or supported children. Eighteen final
focused tests pass 3,927 assertions, including four orientations, thirty-two
placement seeds, split-chunk identity, wet/public exclusion, actual cap support,
closed collision and grass containment. Earlier intermediate logs are retained
but are not final acceptance evidence.

A controlled actual-character experiment rejected 8 m rises: only 20 of 24
approaches reached their target. Limiting each climbable rise to complete 2 m
or 4 m native stock passes all 24 approaches with zero underside contacts.
These are separate approaches to each shelf, not a continuous tour. The first
uncontrolled harness also walked off small caps; its failures were not credited
as geometry defects. Six independent approaches in a fresh P18 production world
also pass, three levels from both sides, with zero underside contacts.

All fifteen final native before/after pairs were inspected at P15, P17 and P18:
original reconstructed camera, two nearby headings, side and wide overview.
Nine fresh P18 game captures were also inspected: three original/nearby views
and six jump endpoints. Fifteen local grass tiles contain 370 verified native
cap roots. Fresh startup took 357.757 seconds; no performance improvement is
claimed. Two invalid launch attempts are excluded: one scene type mismatch and
one missing offscreen capture setup. The final harness requires a valid capture
mode. The snapshot helper's editor-only global-parameter enumeration warning
is separate from the valid captured game frames.

The wider stacked shelves are retained as a partial improvement. Wide vistas
still show repetitive cliff walls, and these groups do not yet make long
continuous terrace paths. Issue 28 therefore remains partial; cliff composition,
large rocks/vegetation and landscape relief remain open.
