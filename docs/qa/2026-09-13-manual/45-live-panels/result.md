# P10: complete visual batches before review capture

The additional missing panels discovered during issue 09 came from the review harness freezing the world before its main-thread feature visual queue finished. Production collision readiness intentionally arrives before visual completion. An idle terrain worker and three seconds of stable collision were insufficient evidence that every ordered visual batch had been committed.

The shared village capture harness now also waits for an empty feature commit queue before capturing or saving the frozen world. Production streaming, readiness and rendering are unchanged. The regression drives the real FeatureCommitQueue through collision-only, partially committed and fully committed stages. The original predicate fails two assertions; the corrected predicate plus the existing commit-queue tests pass eight tests / 40 assertions. See `red.txt`, `focused.txt` and `test_september13_capture_readiness.gd`.

A mesh-resource/transform inventory of the old issue-09 snapshot finds 70 absent wall/window pieces among 323 expected wall pieces. Their batches lie in the uncommitted tail. The already completed issue-39 world contains all 345 current expected wall/window pieces. A fresh P10 run with the corrected harness again contains all 345, including matching world positions and basis vectors within 0.001. The fresh inventory has 906 other-and-wall matches; 29 non-wall mismatches include re-materialized cliff lips, barrel placements and two pillars. Those are not counted as missing walls or independently cleared by this investigation. See `mesh-inventory.json`, `current-inventory.json` and `fresh-inventory.json`.

The fresh run takes 286.793 seconds to reach the capture. Original ReviewCam P10 and ±8-degree captures show the complete facades. All three saved-world replays also retain those facades. The comparison sheet was inspected at all angles. Older-to-current differences include intervening accepted village changes; the old snapshot cannot serve as a complete-world or isolated-change reference. The frozen replay's atmosphere and the independent grass queue differ from the fresh capture, so these views do not establish grass or lighting acceptance. Geometry inventories and the queue regression isolate the panel discrepancy.

This closes issue 45 as a review-harness correction, not a production renderer repair. Issue 09's native bay-back attachment evidence remains valid; its old incomplete live worlds must not be used to claim complete facade materialization. No new production corpus is required for this harness-only change; the latest issue-42 production sweep remains 48/48 towns and 95/95 composition assertions. General streaming performance and unrelated rendering defects are not accepted here.
