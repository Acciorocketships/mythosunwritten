# Circled mountain cut-outs

Source: [owner annotation](owner-annotated.png).

| Marked region | Cause | Repair / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Left circle, roughly pixels 0–220 × 280–450 | A narrow wet pocket was treated as a channel that must excavate the hillside; face grades reached 74–79°. | Require an 8 m water-core radius before forcing a receiver cut. Actual-site regression and native front/side views. |
| Large central outline, roughly pixels 720–1360 × 170–340 | The same receiver cut punched two deep bowls through the rounded slope, with 77–80° faces and up to 5.51 m drop per horizontal metre. | Restore the rounded envelope over narrow wet pockets while retaining the broad opposing-bank channel. |
| Upper-right oval, roughly pixels 1210–1530 × 90–160 | Separate bedrock carving recessed a bench 1.25 m into the hillside. | Limit inward rock carving to 0.25 m; retain outward rock relief and the original material. Four exact-site checks reproduce and fix it. |
| Additional false dark recesses found in neutral controls | Sparse-field classification sentinels were incorrectly differentiated as distances to shade the surface. | Derive bedrock normals from the complete height envelope. Native plateau regression fails before and passes after. |

Material-only softening was rejected as insufficient. The final build retains the original moss palette and blend.
