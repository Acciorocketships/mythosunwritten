Rock asset catalog

308 entries · 34 contact sheets · two opposite views of every asset.
Original geometry and materials as imported into Godot. No slope deformation or game moss shader. Models are scaled to fit each frame; labels show native bounds (width × height × depth).

The newest packs come first: Meadow (ANGRY MESH), Farmlands (Polyart), and Suntail (Raygeas). Seasonal variants follow. Older libraries and project-generated meshes are below. Alternate export formats and KayKit palette duplicates are omitted; architectural stone walls, fences and props are not treated as landscape rocks.

Use the IDs when giving feedback, for example M01, P03, R06. Click a sheet for its full-resolution screenshot.

Meadow material correction: the original pack layers terrain grass onto upward rock faces (dry grass in autumn, snow in winter). That shader layer was lost during conversion and is missing in these imported-material screenshots. Material findings.
