# Unified city, supported gardens, and connected skywalk height

Accepted for the photographed defects and measured corpus. Ground houses and
upper warren rooms now come from one source allocation. The separate outskirts
house/ring pass is removed; actual world-road handoffs connect to source gates.
Low lobes in the same massif provide ground neighborhoods before public routes,
native prefabs and modular plots reserve their space. Court and open-bay exclusions
remain part of that source field.

The owner’s two follow-ups were handled individually. Retained earth over public
air now needs a continuous crown between two real opposing jambs. The photographed
floating turf, stone and timber bed disappears completely. A first candidate that
left four floating stone cells was rejected. Skywalk admission now requires a
complete neighboring wall storey beside each endpoint foundation group, outside
the span’s own compound and clear of future public openings. The photo town keeps
one connected interior span instead of two isolated perimeter spans. Three low
native prefabs remain, versus two in the old core and four in the initial proposal.

A seed-8 grand-town roof failure exposed inconsistent early/final roof domains.
Complete exposed crowns now consume the same measured native tight alternatives
reserved before placement, despite stale flat topology markers. Explicit bridge
party profiles remain intact. No asset rescaling, overlap tolerance, seed exception,
whole-town retry or timing-pin change was introduced.
