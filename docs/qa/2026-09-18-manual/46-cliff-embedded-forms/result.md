# Embedded-form experiments — not adopted

These fixture-only trials precede the owner's explicit selection of the older yellow-world and oblique tall-wall references. Production was not changed.

`embedded` shortens the Nature-derived vertical masses and adds oblique connected stone relief. `depth` varies the exposure with elevation; `fanstone` broadens descending supports; `bearing` preserves usable ledge width by combining body depths instead of summing them into the projection compressor. Each seven-test gate passes six tests / twelve of thirteen assertions, with the unchanged large-outline repetition diagnostic still failing. None is accepted artistically: tall vertical supports and thin or plain shelves remain visible.

The actual upper-tread regression is red on the before control: 3.992243 m² of broad upper turf, 15.02% of sampled upper turf. The bearing trial yields 19.364041 m² and 54.47%, passing two assertions. This is a measured local improvement, not acceptance of its appearance.

Frozen-world captures rebuild rock and plants on saved terrain; they do not regenerate world grass, water admission, collision traversal or streaming.
