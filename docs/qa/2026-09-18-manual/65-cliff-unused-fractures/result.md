# Unused fracture computation removed

Production no longer computes the retired small-fracture profile for every sampled cliff column. The existing body builder never consumes this array. The legacy argument and helper remain available to historical study fixtures; the current path passes an empty array.

The saved before and lean sources were prepared once and compared over all 31 photographed formations plus 16, 32 and 64 m walls, with two repetitions and alternating execution order. All 68 complete output dictionaries are byte-identical, including visible geometry, turf, collision and metadata. The comparison reports 33,190.745 ms before and 32,646.820 ms after. This is a local aggregate measurement under concurrent work, not a startup or streaming performance claim.

Eight focused production tests / fifteen assertions pass. A native Metal replay of the fixed P12 front camera was inspected. It retains the existing broad plain faces and lower turf artifacts; the cleanup does not fix or claim to fix their appearance. No new geometry candidate is promoted, and no original judging issue is closed.

The frozen replay retains its saved terrain, atmosphere, grass and collision. No new full-world generation or physical traversal was necessary for this exact-output cleanup. Broader cliff art remains open.
