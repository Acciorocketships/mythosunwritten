# Curved projection study — not promoted

The pass-60 source is frozen in `tests/fixtures/september18/cliff-curved-descent/before.gd`. Production has not changed. These candidates retain the larger, shallow geometry-linked divisions and vary only the full-height projection envelope.

The stronger quadratic growth (`curved`) holds the upper wall closer to its backing and retains its root. It passes eight of nine focused tests / sixteen of seventeen assertions, but reduces the connected upper-ledge count from five to three and the maximum span from 40.25 to 34 m. It is rejected rather than weakening the ledge count guard.

The weaker curve (`gentle`) passes the existing nine focused tests / seventeen assertions: the 57 cap probes retain turf, photographed solids remain closed, and four upper ledges span 6.25–36.5 m. Maximum photographed lip recession is 0.067 m with no breaches. Seventeen frozen world views and five tall study views were captured. Inspected P12 front/side, P17 front and tall oblique views retain plain large faces; the tall composition looks flatter. It is not selected.

A new experimental comparison guard requires at least 3 cm mean inward change in the upper quarter while preserving the actual base. The before source fails with zero change. The gentle trial also fails: only 0.008931 m mean retreat over 25,339 common outline samples, with no outward movement and exactly unchanged 1,158 foot samples. This is evidence that the change is too small to justify the shelf loss, not a claim that centimetres alone determine visual quality. This guard remains in the study fixture and is not registered as a production requirement.

The envelope is not the remaining composition fix: further limiting it mostly flattens existing rock shapes. The next investigation must address the combined large rock body and shelf-support construction while retaining the crown, root and ledge invariants. No fresh world or physical traversal is claimed. The frozen images retain their original terrain/grass/collision. The original judging register remains open.

The completed integration run passes all thirteen grass/corner tests, including 358 fully supported grass patches, closed 16/32/64 m corner solids, worker safety, complete public/wet exclusions and independent chunk ownership. Including the experimental retreat guard, the run is 13/14 tests and 52/53 assertions in 230.140 s. These safety results do not reverse the visual rejection. All study processes have exited.
