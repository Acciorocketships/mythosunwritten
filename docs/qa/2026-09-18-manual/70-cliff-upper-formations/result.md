# Physical upper-rock studies — unselected

Production remains pass 69. No renderer, material, collision or production geometry change is promoted in this pass. This work is progress through falsification: actual geometry variants were rendered in the same native cameras, and their remaining defects rule out several approaches. The original judging register remains open.

## What the studies establish

The existing root-derived projection envelope flattens some added profiles before the final mesh is built. Relaxing their vertical fade makes upper formations physically measurable, but produces dark short cuts and shallow embossing. Softening the envelope join removes some cuts without producing sufficiently distinct rock composition. More profile density is not an adequate repair.

Removing only the ledge-support function leaves upright/fluted rock bodies. Removing only the full-height Nature profile contribution also leaves upright support organization, along with a much plainer upper face. Neither is independently the entire cause. The next structural investigation must consider their combined exterior rather than treating one amplitude as the solution.

A late physical displacement of finite, rotated Nature fronts makes bumps visible in the normal game views, but reads as small embossed patches. Complete original rock triangles, placed on a single inclined bearing plane rather than draped vertex-by-vertex over the wall, retain clearer boulder silhouettes but still have conspicuous contact edges. These are useful geometry controls, not accepted art. They do not justify replacing the production wall with attached lumps.

## Physical regression evidence

`upper-test.gd` compares actual front vertices in a 96 m by 64 m wall, seed 2697992464, pose origin (-13.5, 0, 10.5). Its upper window is 32–58 m and the crown control is above 63 m. It fails on unchanged production with zero moved vertices and zero affected columns (three of six assertions fail). A shading-only change cannot pass.

The exposed variant passes all six assertions: 16,766 of 52,094 samples project over 0.15 m across 349 columns; 29,426 remain quiet; maximum gain is 0.873700 m; crown change is zero. The separate soft-joins variant passes twelve tests / 28 assertions, including the new physical test, 57/57 turf cap probes, 31 closed shells and zero upper-envelope excess over 304,210 samples. Its physical test measures 14,496 projected samples, 337 columns, 26,770 quiet samples, maximum gain 0.701600 m and zero crown movement. Those green checks are not attributed to the other variants and do not override the adverse visual judgment.
