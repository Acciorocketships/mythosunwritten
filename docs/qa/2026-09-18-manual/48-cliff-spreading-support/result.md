# Support and joint diagnosis — rejected experiments

No source from this study is promoted. The owner has since rejected small fractures in randomly selected areas; the preferred direction is larger, shallower divisions associated with actual rock shapes and ledge risers.

The control is pass 47 `long`. Its post-column downward maximum carries upper projections down as nearly straight columns. Removing that operation (`free`) lowers the unchanged macro-outline correlation from 0.763078 to 0.330485, but does not establish physical lower support. It is an ablation, not an acceptable unsupported replacement.

Faster, broader widening feet do not fix the column composition while that envelope remains. `spread` measures 0.773670; additive `spreading` 0.822312; max-union shoulders without the final clamp measure 0.848301. All fail the unchanged 0.65 limit. The failed initial `union` parser runs remain in the record and are not geometry evidence.

Seven native close-view controls separate geometry, shadow, ambient occlusion and shading. The serrated fracture edges remain with shadows disabled. A recessed-floor profile (`clefts`) reduces the needle-like V-shaped artifacts in one native ledge view, but does not remove all rough edges. It has no quantitative or production acceptance and does not override the owner's subsequent rejection of the small-patch approach.

This investigation also reproduced a separate upper-metre projection bug in actual production: 2.692877 m of added projection. The scoped follow-up is recorded in [pass 49](../49-cliff-crown-collar/result.md).
