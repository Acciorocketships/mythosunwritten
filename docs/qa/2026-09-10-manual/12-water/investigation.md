Options considered:

- Change only normals/material: rejected as insufficient; measured geometry has real height discontinuities in slope.
- Force all connected water to the lowest level: would drain valid upstream reaches and disconnect flow on rising beds.
- Raise terrain to match water: hides the symptom and changes walkable ground and habitats.
- Reconcile the complete already-wet surface downward toward lower connected heads, with a bounded gradient and a terrain floor: candidate. It must preserve existing dry boundaries, enclosed lakes, and real terrain barriers. Fine rescue and rim geometry need separate falsification after the coarse field is corrected.

The first regression pins three photographed flat-ground transitions, requires both endpoints to stay wet, and limits the rise to 0.75 m over 3 m. This is a necessary check, not sufficient visual acceptance.
