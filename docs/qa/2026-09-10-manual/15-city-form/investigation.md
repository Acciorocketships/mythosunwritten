# City form and cohesion — investigation

The user explicitly requests broader 3D shape variation and unified prefab/modular generation, superseding the older round inhabited-mountain and exterior-ring presentation constraints. Structural support, deterministic ownership, native asset bounds, connected traversable streets and correct final surfaces remain requirements.

Current causes: `WarrenMassifBuilder` selects only one warped Gaussian footprint; its route score and summit stop reference the origin. `WarrenMassif.validate_construction` rejects every enclosed hole, including an intentional courtyard. `VillageOutskirtsConstruction` allocates houses on four sides of a bounding rectangle after the urban plan. Source prefab reservations do participate in the modular plan, but their minimum-cut cost and limited supported interior rectangles admit few assets and often the same compact family.

Options considered: (1) change only noise/height ranges — insufficient to create courts or change the separate-ring composition; (2) crop or lower the finished city — breaks the already assigned routes/supports; (3) author varied complete source footprints and open-ground courts before streets/plots, then let both prefab and modular buildings own supported street-fronting reservations. Proceed with source ownership and preserve the normal final construction transaction. Baseline full four-scale corpus and matched source/game views precede implementation.

## Owner's additional direction: small settlements and landmarks

The follow-up requests connected T roofs, a smaller default, genuinely tiny ground settlements, low edges, and asset-informed variation. The warmth reference is a compositional reference rather than a source of instructions. Its attractive qualities are the readable houses, gathering space and large trees; its scattered frontages, weak path hierarchy and crowded centre should not be copied.

Asset inventory confirms native SFV wells, the BattlePack campfire, one SFV windmill assembly (tower, rotor and assembled version), six watermill pieces, an SFV bridge, LPFV trees and mill parts, SFV bisected roof tiles and corner gables, and a MegaKit timber corner roof. Wells/campfire, trees and bridges already have runtime descriptors. Windmills/watermill parts need measured catalogue preparation before they may enter worker construction. An asset's presence on disk does not by itself establish collision or a usable entrance.
