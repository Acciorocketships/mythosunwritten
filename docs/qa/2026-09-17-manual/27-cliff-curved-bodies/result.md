# Curved body investigations — rejected

These studies retained the saved production context and ReviewCam poses while regenerating the cliff dressing. Each directory contains 17 native renderer captures. P05, P12 and P20 views were inspected across the studies.

- `candidate/`: increased lateral mass drift. The body still read as rounded boxes; insufficient visual improvement.
- `rooted-flare/`: wider lower shoulders increased the base, but retained soft boxy masses and repetition.
- `nature-priority/`: stronger source-rock influence produced sharp teeth and visible seams.
- `independent-skin/`: narrower native-wall inheritance with rotated, stronger detail produced soft sheets and angular notches.
- `coverage/`: diagnostic thin-to-independent coverage colours confirmed that the added surface already covers most of the wall. Missing coverage alone does not explain the poor composition.

None of these shape candidates was promoted. No test, performance or overall art acceptance is claimed. The subsequent ledge-channel repair is documented separately in [pass 28](../28-cliff-ledge-channels/result.md).
