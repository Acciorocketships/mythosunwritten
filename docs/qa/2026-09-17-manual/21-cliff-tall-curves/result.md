# Tall cliff curves: rejected experiments

Production remains the preceding inclined-broad-terrace revision, byte-identical to `tests/fixtures/september17/cliff-tall-curves/before.gd`. No current test thresholds were changed and none of these art candidates were promoted.

The 64 m native corner control still shows repeated vertical ribs and horizontal courses. Three isolated experiments generated native views:

1. Lateral mass drift and finite winding vertical seams. This modestly changes the physical cross-section but still reads as elongated ribs. The first render uses the source retained as `rendered-first.gd`; the later `candidate.gd` widens the source-admission envelope to account for the drift. The latter is used by the numeric probe, not claimed as the exact source of the initial images.
2. Extending complete Nature body profiles up tall walls (`full-height.gd`). Their contribution is largely dominated by the existing mass envelope; the visible effect is insufficient.
3. Restricting inherited native geometry and normals to a narrower attachment interval (`attachment.gd`, `attachment-corner.gd`). The repeated structure remains apparent, and the stronger shape separation does not achieve the desired integrated rock face.

The front images of all three were inspected against the preceding 64 m control. Five views were generated per experiment. These are studio controls, not new reported-pose game replays or proof of collision/streaming acceptance. Since the candidates failed visual selection, no new production gate is based on them.

The detached measurement probe (`measure.gd`, [measure.log](measure.log)) compares physical depth profiles eight metres apart after subtracting each row's mean. Variation increases in several slices, but that metric is insufficient: the native image still shows the unwanted vertical organization. Increased numeric variability alone must not be treated as an art improvement.

The next cliff work must change the persistent mass/attachment composition more substantially; another small groove-noise adjustment is not supported by these results. Broader cliff art remains open. The ongoing water investigation is recorded separately in [10-hillside-order/result.md](../10-hillside-order/result.md).
