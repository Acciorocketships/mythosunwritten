# Rooted fan and finite-body composition studies

Four new shape studies were rendered and rejected. None changes the selected production composition. The preceding groove comparison is progress on surface treatment; these experiments address the separate repeated-column problem.

| Study | Construction | Native judgment |
|---|---|---|
| `fans` | Shorter authored bodies, generic masses spreading and deepening with descent, quieter lateral modulation | Tall front remains an extruded facade. Reported amber cliff changes little; P05 lower steps look heavier. |
| `terraced` | Every generic ledge gains a widening support fan, with broader selected upper terraces | Sloping transitions become visible but upright organization persists. Lower connections merge into a broad soft facade. |
| `basal` | Selected broad feet added only below the lower 55% of the wall | More local basal projection, but too little additional rock identity. Retains broad smooth faces. |
| `stacked` | Independent finite authored bodies overlap in height; disables the generic extruded ledges/masses to isolate their effect | Clearly changes the composition, but makes large block-like panels with unsupported undersides and removes too much turf. Rejected both visually and physically. |

Reviewed native views include tall oblique, P20 oblique and P05 reported camera 0 for `fans`, `terraced` and `stacked`; P20 oblique, P12 side and P05 reported camera 0 for `basal`. Full retained counts: 22 views each for `fans`, `terraced`, `stacked` (17 frozen context + five 64 m studio), and 17 frozen context views for `basal`. No fresh-world, grass regeneration, traversal or global performance acceptance.

## Physical checks

`fans` and `basal` each pass the six reported ledge/support tests / twelve assertions. No equivalent pass is claimed for `terraced`, which was rejected by native judgment before broader testing. `stacked` passes four of six tests / nine of twelve assertions: straight-wall recession reaches 5.1773 m, corner recession reaches 3.4058 m, and broad ledge area falls to 64.657 m² below the retained 80 m² requirement. Its channel, turf contact and photo-shell checks alone do not establish acceptable rock support.

## Finding

Extending a ledge's nearly constant lateral outline down the full height produces columns even with local surface grooves. Widening all those supports together preserves too much of the same facade. The finite-body trial confirms that limiting body height changes the dominant organization, but its independent bases are not a valid structural composition. Future body work needs compatible overlapping bearings and ledges derived from the composed bodies; making every new shape an independently supported extrusion is not enough.

The [groove integration trial](../41-cliff-selected-grooves/result.md) was subsequently withdrawn after broader corner and grass regressions. It does not claim these large-form requirements are solved.
