# Rejected shallow chipped-face experiment

The baseline remains the triangulation repair plus the canopy-spacing correction. A detached generator candidate added irregular shallow polygonal chips to thick stone, fading with the existing thin-attachment weight. Production CliffRockCrags was not edited.

Seventeen native context views completed. P20 oblique and P12 front were inspected against the exact preceding context. The changes mostly create small wrinkles; the broad faces still read too softly, and the detail does not establish a more convincing hierarchy of fractured stone. This is not a sufficient visual improvement and is **rejected**. No green test result or art acceptance is claimed for the candidate.

Source remains in `tests/fixtures/september17/cliff-weathering/candidate.gd`; exact baseline is `before.gd` beside it. Native output is in `candidate/`, with `render.log`. The next geometry study should address medium-scale cut faces and their transitions, rather than adding more small surface noise. Full-height coverage, wider selected bases and native thin-root blending remain in production from prior repairs.

A second candidate deepened the chips (0.28–0.60 m) and narrowed neighboring-normal averaging to 25–45 degrees. Seventeen more native views completed. P20 gains sharper cuts, but P12 front exposes objectionable triangle patches on the near lower face and crown. It is also rejected, remaining only in `candidate2.gd` and `candidate2/`. Production geometry stays byte-identical to this study's baseline. Neither experiment resolves the remaining art issue.
