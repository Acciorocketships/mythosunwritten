# Curved-tread recheck after support repair — rejected

The genuine grass-border repair in [pass 24](../24-cliff-mass-support/result.md) justified rechecking the previously rejected depthwise curved treads. Neither experiment is selected. Production remains pass 24's continuous feet with the existing inclined broad treads and along-wall curves.

`candidate.gd` combines the corrected mass field with quarter-depth eased treads, stitched by tread fraction rather than elevation. It measures 242 curved photo columns, but retains only five fully supported grass roots: **3/4 tests, 8/9 assertions**, [probe.log](probe.log). Seventeen native context views with the matching corner generator are in [candidate/](candidate/); P12_side was inspected against pass 24. The visible gain is slight, and the larger vertical organization remains.

`clipped.gd` preserves the original inner 60% of each cap's triangle planes and bends its outer portion. Under the new whole-component plane-fit proof it retains only two eligible grass patches: **3/4 tests, 8/9 assertions**, [clipped-probe.log](clipped-probe.log). It is not accepted or claimed visually verified. No production geometry or tests were altered for these trials, and no support thresholds were weakened.

Further work needs to address the actual organization of rock shapes and viable planting on curved surfaces. Numerical curvature alone is not evidence that the cliff meets the owner's reference.
