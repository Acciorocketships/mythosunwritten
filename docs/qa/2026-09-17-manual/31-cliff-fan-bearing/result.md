## Art investigation

Nine fixture families were explored: wider generic bearing fans, narrower inherited wall relief, sharp and worn positive crags, max-envelope masses, elliptical masses, authored bearing profiles and taller authored profiles. Their native context captures and fixture sources are retained here and in the matching fixture directory. P20 oblique and P12 side views were inspected for each. The worn variant also received P05 and P17 inspection. None met the requested combination of irregular rocky forms, broad grounded terraces and coherent wall attachment.

The authored-bearing candidate has more variable lower widths, but a native 64 m tall study exposes excessive repeated upper backing. A fresh P20 world completed terrain, feature and grass generation, producing three saved camera captures; the central view was inspected. It still looks too smooth, with thin upper ledges. Its startup was 438.962 seconds under concurrent work, not a performance comparison. The runtime snapshot helper also emitted its known editor-only global-parameter-list diagnostic.

## Candidate failures

The authored-bearing combined run is **49/52 tests, 2,687/2,690 assertions**, not a pass. It loses coverage at a photographed corner, upper tall-corner thickness, and sufficient distinct projections. The widened-source-foot diagnostic reproduces the old narrow-foot condition (zero of three qualifying profiles); the authored candidate widens twelve of twelve profiles. Those experimental authored-foot and continuity checks are archived as study scripts because that field replacement is not selected. The initial area diagnostic was already green on the preceding geometry and is explicitly not red regression evidence.

## Retained test improvement

The P05 channel test now locates the actual tread depth at three fixed photographed columns before casting 19 interior rays per column. This preserves the reported shelf while allowing its geometry to move. The archived defective `ordered-first.gd` has turf at only **30/57** contacts; restored production covers **57/57**. The old nine fixed-depth rays could miss a shelf that moved inward and incorrectly report air as a defect. No material or geometric acceptance threshold was loosened.

Final native colour verification passes **one GPU test / 12 assertions** (`colour-gpu.log`). All three controlled native/outcrop seams have zero measured colour mismatch. The final geometry follow-up passes **14 tests / 45 assertions** across the ledge-channel, corner-joint and projection checks (`restored-tests-retry.log`); the first invocation failed before tests during Godot log rotation, so it was rerun with an explicit independent engine log.

The unrelated existing blank-line-at-EOF warning in `tests/fixtures/legacy_room_repair.gd` was not modified. No full-suite, traversal, global performance or broader original-issue acceptance is claimed.
