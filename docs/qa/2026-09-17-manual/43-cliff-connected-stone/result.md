# Connected rock geometry studies — unselected

The owner rejects the earlier tall-wall columns and isolated scratch segments. Passes 39/41 are no longer a preferred art direction. These studies instead derive worn volumes and optional joints from the same irregular two-dimensional stone partition. The joint is physical geometry and shares the surface/collision triangles; the quiet shared stone shader supplies no crack texture.

## Rejected and retained experiments

- `subtle`: connected polygon relief breaks the tapered rectangle grid, but reads as oversized cobbling.
- `weathered` / `weathered-clean`: narrower borrowing of the native backing and quieter patches reduce repetition. The broad lower photo ledges and support checks regress (three of six tests pass).
- `borne`: restores Nature-derived broad lower shelves and constrains each vertical column against every higher projection. All six checks pass, but native views again show tall flat columns. Rejected visually.
- `local`: the support envelope can retreat gradually with descent, so it provides nearby bearing without extruding an upper outline through the entire cliff. Initial allowance 0.75 m fails the new local two-metre support probe. Final allowance 0.50 m passes it. This final source is shown in `final-subtle`, `final-clean`, `final-joints`, and `context`.

The stronger final joints outline the polygons too heavily. Subtle and joint-free versions are quieter, but neither is accepted. The final frozen game replay exposes excessively plain faces on ordinary 4–12 m cliffs. Its fixed 8 m stone partition and 10 m shelf pitch are inappropriate at that scale. Further work is in [the scale study](../../2026-09-18-manual/44-cliff-scale-study/result.md).

## Verification and limits

The final combined run is **8/9 tests, 16/17 assertions** (`local-final-tests.log`). All 31 photographed shells are closed/nondegenerate; 57/57 measured ledge contacts retain turf. Broad tread area is 241.981632 square metres. Crown excess is zero. The old full-height straight recession assertion remains red at 1.037200 m versus 0.85 m; it has not been relaxed in production. A coherent long taper can exceed that criterion without an immediate unsupported belly, so the separate diagnostic checks local bearing too. It measures a maximum 0.820000 m recession within two metres on 1,632 photo columns, with positive and negative controls.

The tall outline diagnostic is red on production (mean adjacent-elevation profile correlation 0.907952) and green on the final study (0.341187). This isolates repeated outlines; it is not a beauty metric. The precise mapped-corner closure check passes one test / nine assertions at 16, 32 and 64 m. This is not the full corner suite.

Five fixed studio views exist for each final joint strength. Seventeen final context views rebuild formations and resample plants in the saved game snapshot. They do not regenerate world grass, establish live traversal, or measure streaming/performance. Production geometry and material remain byte-identical to pre-trial pass 34, which is itself not artistically accepted. No study is promoted.
