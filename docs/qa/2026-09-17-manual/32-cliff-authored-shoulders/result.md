# Authored shoulder studies and zero-depth blend repair

## Selected repair

An admitted authored rock profile with zero depth previously raised the surrounding body. The fixed-radius smooth maximum contributed up to 0.1125 m even when both inputs were zero; successive empty profiles could accumulate more. This contradicts the intended taper where an outcrop enters the cliff.

The blend radius now shrinks with the smaller actual shape depth, reaching zero when either shape vanishes. Existing deep overlaps retain the 0.45 m smoothing radius. This changes the physical generated surface and its collision, not only its shading. Production otherwise retains pass 30 shape formulas, curved treads, pointed turf and shared gray colour.

The new regression first fails both tests and all four assertions (`edge-red.log`). One, two and four empty profiles incorrectly lift the isolated body by 0.11369, 0.17767 and 0.25026 m. An infinitesimal incoming profile produces a 0.113713 m jump. After the fix, empty profiles are neutral and the maximum step over the fine incoming-depth sweep is 0.000040412 m. These isolate the production body formula; they are not measured whole-wall pixel differences.

The combined final geometry run passes **18 tests / 54 assertions** (`edge-tests.log`), including closed/nondegenerate photo shells, 57/57 P05 turf contacts, photographed and tall corner relief, distinct rock projections, lower bearing and crown clearance.
