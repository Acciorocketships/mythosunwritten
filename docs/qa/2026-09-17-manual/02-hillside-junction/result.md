W01 remains open. The photographed upper river (-2,-1) crosses lower river (-3,-4), but its greater hash priority excludes the physically lower receiver. Baseline retains all 288 stations instead of joining before station 50; the retained red case passes only one of three assertions.

The attempted change admitted physically lower receivers irrespective of hash, retaining hash only to break equal-level ties. An early raw-route intersection filter bounded neighbor expansion. At depth one it found the desired join and shortened the upper river to 44 stations. At the production depth two, however, the receiving river had itself joined elsewhere and its downstream reach disappeared. The upper river returned to 288 unjoined stations. The receiving trace similarly alternated between 305 and 34 stations. The test still passed only one of three assertions, and its elapsed time rose from 2.376 to 33.343 seconds.

**Rejected.** WaterPlan was restored byte-for-byte to the saved starting source. RiverTrace and WaterField were not edited in this attempt. The rejected code, topology logs and isolated red fixture are retained; the failing investigation fixture is not in the normal passing-test list.

A correct repair must reconcile tributary ownership with retained receiving reaches, then verify the water-surface descent at the actual junction. Merely reversing the local priority cannot establish a stable network and must not be credited as fixing the photographed pool.
