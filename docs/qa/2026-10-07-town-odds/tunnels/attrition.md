# Tunnel attrition (tunnel chance 1.0)

Generated by `tests/harness/tunnel_attrition.gd`; counters are diagnostics only (fingerprint unchanged: FINGERPRINT_MATCH).

## Counters in plain words

- `walk_cells`: interior cells of every street walk the carver visited (first and last cell of a walk are skipped).
- `eligible`: cells where the massif could carry a bore (`_tunnel_bore` returned a bore). Cells skipped by the run cap are not tested and not counted here.
- `rolled_out`: a run start refused by the seeded roll (0 at chance 1.0).
- `daylight_gap`: a run start refused because the previous run had no daylight cell after it.
- `run_capped`: cells where a run already had 3 bored cells, so no bore was tried.
- `bored_cells`: tunnel cells after carving (includes gate covers and authored wall tunnels, so it can exceed `eligible`).
- `pruned_cells`: tunnel cells removed later when destination pruning dropped optional lanes.
- `final_tunnel_cells`: tunnel cells left when plots are laid.
- `tunnel_covers`: records returned by `cover_tunnels` (one per tunnel cell, success or not).
- `covered`: records with no refusal reason, i.e. a house storey really was placed over the crown.
- `released_crown_cells`: stone cells later released as unborne crowns (`maze_released_unborne_crown_cells`, whole-town).

## Largest loss

Of 107 tunnel cells that survive carving and pruning, only 16 (15%) end up with a house storey over them: `cover_tunnels` (`WarrenPlotPlanner.gd:1141`) refuses 91. The refusals, in order: the crown column already carries another plot (30, `:1161`), the passage walls do not bear on two jambs (29, `:1165`), no adjacent house storey exists to extend over the crown (20, `:1169`), no crown stone (5), would overtop the citadel plinth (4), edge-ring or support-rule rejections (3). The remaining 85% of bored cells are the "tunnels that do not read as covered passages", and the 340 released unborne crown cells show the stone above them is later removed. This is the largest loss among tunnel cells.

Upstream, the carve stage is the second loss: only 98 of 694 walk cells (14%) can be bored at all. The seeded roll (`TUNNEL_START_CHANCE` 1.0), the daylight rule (`MIN_DAYLIGHT_RUN`) and the run cap (`MAX_TUNNEL_RUN` 3) cost almost nothing (0, 0 and 4 cells); the losses are the physical tests in `_tunnel_bore` (`WarrenMazeCarver.gd:1887`): straight/turn geometry only, flights and bridged cells excluded, headroom, and above all the massif needing `top_at >= roof + 2` over the bore width and solid jamb columns (`:1924` onward). `_tunnel_bore` has no per-reason counter, so the split among those tests is not measured here. Destination pruning removes only 2 cells.

Caveats: 12 towns; `walk_cells` includes cells on open-sky streets the massif never covers, so 14% is an upper bound on what is realistically boreable. The `covered` figure is cells with a PLOT_OVER, not whole tunnels.
