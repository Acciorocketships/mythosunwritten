# City furnishings — accepted

The existing native bench, bucket, crate and sack assets now furnish complete
private garden-edge stations. Two cells hold a bench and bucket; a single cell
can hold a crate with a sack resting on its measured lid. Lamp stations claim
their space first. Public turf, entrances and occupied air remain excluded,
and later incidental planting consumes only the remaining space. At most four
stations are built per town; each group fits its complete reserved rectangle.

The first same-level walk-adjacency experiment added no furniture to the three
photographed sources. The accepted private-edge layout adds two props in the
east town and six in the offset town. It replaces one old ground-level lantern,
for a net gain of seven props. The thin-turf town has no available private
garden cells and receives none. [Asset review](asset-review.md) records the
source search and why larger firewood/stall prefabs were not fitted into these
small spaces. Source construction and circulation are unchanged.

The sack is raised 0.7283466 m from the crate origin, derived from sampled
native lid/base triangles. Using the crate's bounding-box top would leave it
hovering over the recessed lid. The geometry samples and source hashes are
saved with the regression evidence.

Six matched production views at photo 2 and five native-town gallery views,
including amplified pixel differences, are visually accepted. Crates have
supported bases, sacks contact the lids, the bench legs meet the green and
the bucket clears the bench and nearby barrel. Existing walls, flowers,
lamps and walkways remain readable. The production exact-view mean absolute
RGB difference is 0.8063; 0.9101% of pixels change by more than 20 in a channel.
Minor orb/character animation contributes temporal pixels. Camera records match.

Seven tests pass with 543 assertions (26.351 s). The six-town decor clearance
regression passes with 39 assertions (26.159 s). The actual physical survey
is identical across 124 walk cells and 179 crossings. The independent native
town gallery omits natural surroundings and the avatar; production captures
provide the in-world check. Its initial duplicate support submission was found,
removed and all five pairs were refreshed and rejudged. Rejected gallery
outputs remain labelled; corrected timings and differences are in
`verification.json`. Neither stage establishes a globally green baseline suite.
