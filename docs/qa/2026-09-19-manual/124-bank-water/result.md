# N04 water geometry and wider bank coverage

The frozen production water does not rise above its 13.7 m lake level along
the sampled N04 transects. Six transects at x = -530, -520, -510, -500, -490
and -480, z = 288 through 336, yield 211 double-sided native mesh hits. Heights
range from 13.21159 to 13.7 m; most are exactly 13.7 m. Opaque-water and hidden-
water controls distinguish the actual water surface from the cliff visible
through it. The apparent tall sheet in this view is not evidence of rising
water geometry. This does not close W09 at other bank locations or phases.

The controls select all five frozen water meshes by their actual shader.
An initial name-based selection found only one remote mesh and zero hits;
that run is invalid. The frozen world was saved at N02 and lacks resident grass
at N04. Final controls restore the same four real baseline GrassField payloads
(503 instances) and the local grass LOD origin in every comparison.
Three reconstructed ReviewCam angles retain identical world geometry while
changing only water visibility/material. Diagnostic opacity is not a proposed
production material change.

The existing narrow pass-121 trial was also rendered with all 41 admitted bank
formations, instead of just the 17 nearest the camera. This fills more of the
distant bare underwater wall. Of these, 36 have their complete contact-probe
footprint inside the frozen nine ground owners. They pass 43,366 wet backing
probes, 5,544 buried-foot samples and channel checks three metres outward,
with zero missing backing, floating feet or obstructions. Maximum measured
projection from the native wall is 1.487854 m. Five formations extend beyond
the snapshot; their contacts remain unverified. The initial unrestricted run
counted absent snapshot terrain as failures and is not a valid physical gate.

This wider composition still needs better integration above the waterline;
the native repeated wall remains visible there. It is not final art acceptance.
The wider pass-123 profile was rejected: it adds smooth coverage without usable
exposed grass ledges. The narrow trial remains the comparison baseline.

Production water and bank dressing are unchanged by this investigation.
Independent neighboring water-domain comparison continues in pass 125;
joined recipes, production publication and broader art remain open.
