# Three-way implicit surface — rejected art study

Production remains pass 102. The study combines the actual tall, raised-base and perpendicular formations at (-445.5, 28, -277.5), using a clipped local box in the frozen fresh pass-102 P12 world.

Four reconstructions were rendered: normalized depth, raw depth, triangle-rasterized depth and distances to the actual cross-section segments. All use an isolated marching-tetra surface; none is a production recipe. The first three produce ragged turf and diagonal bands. A bare-rock render confirms that the bands are geometric, not merely turf colour. The distance version reduces the bands but retains coarse edges and does not correct the projecting upper blade. Rejected.

The planar tetra diagnostic produces eight triangles with maximum normal error 0.000008985. It rules out a basic plane winding error, not general reconstruction defects. The final distance study has 699,273 field samples and 237,849 triangles, which also makes this an unattractive replacement for the existing local construction.

The lower-above views of all variants and the bare diagnostic were visually inspected. Other captured angles are retained as evidence, not claimed as judged. All native processes exited normally. No production test, collision, fresh generation, performance or overall cliff acceptance is claimed. The production hashes match pass 102 exactly.

The next study moves the collinear handoff to the taller wall's actual end, above existing supporting terrain, instead of rebuilding the entire three-way neighborhood.
